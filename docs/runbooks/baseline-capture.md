# Performance capture (retired)

The performance/resource capture tooling and its operator procedure have been
retired. There is no supported baseline-capture, Tracy, memory-recorder, or
performance-budget command in this checkout.

Closed milestone measurements, launch logs, review records, and manifests remain
historical evidence, not instructions for a new run. Files retained because a
closed manifest binds their paths are frozen evidence inputs; their digests are
checked at the manifest's pinned revision.

For launch proof, use [`prism-launch.md`](prism-launch.md). Evidence admission
and privacy requirements remain in [`evidence-admission.md`](evidence-admission.md).
