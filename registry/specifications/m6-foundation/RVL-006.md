## 1. What this entry is

Adds a hand-held item that remotely opens a storage network's crafting grid,
powered by a stored energy buffer that drains per action (**read**). The portable
core is the energy affordability and drain model (**read**).

## 2. The energy model

- A buffer with a fixed capacity of **1000** units (**read**).
- Per-action costs (**read**): opening the grid **5**, a craft **5**, an
  autocraft **10**, clearing the matrix **5**, a recipe transfer **5**. The costs
  are configurable but carry those defaults.
- An action proceeds only when the buffer holds at least its cost; on completion
  the cost is drained (**read** — the drain is applied at the transaction's close,
  not at the start).

## 3. What a counterpart implements

The buffer with its capacity, the per-action cost table (configurable, with the
defaults above), an affordability query, and a perform operation that drains on
success and refuses when underfunded, plus a charge operation.

## 4. What this specification does not specify

- **The grid operations themselves, the network item context, and the menu**:
  host concerns.
- **Where energy comes from** (charging source): host concern.
- **Coverage.** Read in full: the capacity, cost table, and the close-time drain.
  Everything else: listed, not read.
