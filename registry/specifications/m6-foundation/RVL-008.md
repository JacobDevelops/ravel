## 1. What this entry is

A set of tweaks around dark-biome skeletons and their skull drops (**read**):

1. **Conversion**: skeletons spawning in the dark dimension — or optionally in all
   dark biomes — become the wither variant on spawn.
2. **Guaranteed skull**: killing a wither-variant skeleton with a sword or a
   firework guarantees a skull drop.
3. **Fragment item**: when no skull drops, a fragment drops instead; fragments
   craft into a full skull.
4. **A special blade**: ignites its target and instantly kills skeletons,
   guaranteeing the skull via the fire kill.
5. **Loot cleanup**: removes stone swords and bows from skeleton drops.

## 2. The portable rules

- **should-convert**: given the spawn context and the "all dark biomes"
  configuration flag, decide whether a skeleton converts (**read**).
- **extra-drops**: given the kill method (sword, firework, fire, other), decide
  whether a guaranteed skull or a fragment is added (**read**).
- **items-to-remove**: the fixed list of vanilla drops stripped from skeleton
  loot, gated on the cleanup configuration (**read**).

## 3. What a counterpart implements

The three decision functions above and the configuration flags they read. It
registers two items, a loot modifier, one item tag, and two save-compatible item
aliases (recorded in the counterpart's aliases file).

## 4. What this specification does not specify

- **Loot-table integration, the fire/ignite effect, and combat hooks**: host
  concerns.
- **Recipes, models, language**: data.
- **Coverage.** Read in full: the conversion, drop, and cleanup rules and their
  configuration gates. Everything else: listed, not read.
