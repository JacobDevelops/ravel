## 1. What this entry is

Adds heart-canister items that raise a player's maximum health (**read**). The
portable core is the tier table and the health arithmetic (**read**).

## 2. The tier table and arithmetic

- Five tiers, four of which carry a heal amount and a multiplier rising with the
  tier, plus one zero-valued tier (**read** — the values are a fixed table: the
  four scaling tiers step the heal amount and the multiplier together, and the
  fifth is zero on both).
- Each stored heart adds **two** points of maximum health — one full heart
  (**read**).
- A canister stores a per-tier count; the total stored-heart count is the sum
  across tiers (**read**).
- **Preserved health on a max change**: when maximum health changes, the missing-
  health gap is held constant — the new current health is `clamp(new_max - gap, 0,
  new_max)` where `gap` was the pre-change `max - current` (**read**).

## 3. What a counterpart implements

The tier table (heal amount, multiplier), the two-per-heart max-health
contribution, the per-tier stored-count sum with normalisation, and the
preserved-health clamp on a max-health change.

## 4. What this specification does not specify

- **Attribute modifiers, equipment slots, and death drops**: host concerns.
- **Coverage.** Read in full: the tier values, the per-heart constant, the sum,
  and the preserved-health formula. Everything else: listed, not read.
