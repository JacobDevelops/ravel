## 1. What this entry is

A decorative content entry adding door blocks in various wood and metal styles
(**read**). It has no gameplay logic beyond registration: the doors behave like
vanilla doors with custom models and sounds; some styles are thinner (a custom
collision shape) and some are multi-block (**read**).

## 2. Content is data, not specification

The set of door variants (style × material) is a catalogue, realised by block/item
registration, models, sounds, tags, and a creative tab (**read**). This document
does not enumerate it: restating a catalogue in prose is the transliteration
failure the clean-room process avoids.

## 3. What a counterpart implements

The door-variant catalogue as data (style, and the registry keys derived from the
mod id), plus the shared vanilla-like door behaviour and the two shape exceptions
(thin styles, multi-block styles) noted as properties of a variant. Registration
is a host concern.

## 4. What this specification does not specify

- **Models, textures, sounds, recipes, tags, creative tab**: data.
- **The exact thin-door shape and multi-block mechanics** (**unknown** beyond
  their existence).
- **Coverage.** Read in part: the catalogue shape and the door behaviour class.
  Per-variant data: seen as directory shape, not read.
