## 1. What this entry is

An enchantment library that stores enchantments as "points" and lets a player
withdraw them onto items (**read**). The portable core is the point economy
(**read**).

## 2. The point economy

- An enchantment level is worth `2^(level-1)` points: level 1 = 1, level 2 = 2,
  level 3 = 4, and so on (**read**).
- Depositing an enchanted book adds that level's points, saturating at the
  library's maximum points, and raises the tracked maximum level for that
  enchantment, capped at the library's maximum level (**read**).
- Withdrawal is permitted when the library holds enough points for the requested
  level given the item's current level, and it debits the points on success
  (**read** — the can-extract/extract pair is the guarded debit).

## 3. What a counterpart implements

The level→points function, and the library with per-enchantment points and tracked
max level, deposit, and the guarded can-extract/extract.
