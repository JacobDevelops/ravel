## 1. What this entry is

A build tool that places many blocks at once, choosing each placed block from a
"pool" (**read**). The portable core is two pool strategies (**read**).

## 2. The two pools

- **ordered pool**: draws elements in insertion order, once each, until reset —
  deterministic patterns (**read**).
- **random pool**: draws weighted without replacement — each draw picks an element
  with probability proportional to its remaining weight and removes it, until the
  pool empties or is reset (**read** — the weighted-without-replacement draw is the
  load-bearing algorithm).

## 3. What a counterpart implements

The pool trait and both implementations: ordered (insertion-order, once each) and
random (weighted, without replacement, over an injected RNG), with remaining-count
and reset.
