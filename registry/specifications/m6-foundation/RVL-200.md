## 1. What this entry is

Integration wiring a recipe-viewer's "move ingredients into the grid" action to a
storage network's crafting grid, resolving each recipe slot against the network
(**read**). The portable core is the transfer classification (**read**).

## 2. The classification

- Per input, availability is one of: available, missing, or autocraftable
  (**read**).
- The overall transfer verdict is derived from the set of per-input availabilities
  — e.g. fully satisfiable, partially missing but autocraftable, or blocked — and
  whether an autocrafting preview may be opened is a function of that verdict
  (**read**).
- When the viewer reports a specific displayed stack for a slot, that stack is
  moved to the front of the slot's candidate list so the transfer prefers it
  (**read** — a stable "prioritise displayed" reorder).

## 3. What a counterpart implements

The per-input and overall transfer enums, the classify function from a list of
per-input availabilities, the can-open-preview predicate, and the prioritise-
displayed reorder.
